Hi, I'm Dan!

I'm a computer scientist, software engineer, and electronic music artist.

I have a PhD in Computer Science from Imperial College London,
where I worked on formal methods and static analysis, and over 10 years of experience developing software professionally. Over the years I've worked on quite a variety of things, including compilers (MLIR/C++), functional programming (Haskell, Clojure), audio software (JUCE) and blockchain.

I've always been fascinated by programming languages, the theory behind them, and the process of turning interesting ideas into working software. I particularly enjoy problems that require a bit of thinking, learning something new, and coming up with elegant solutions.

Outside of programming, music is a big part of my life.
I've been making electronic music for over 20 years, releasing records, collaborating with other artists, and occasionally performing.

Sometimes these two worlds overlap, like when I work on audio software or build little tools to solve my own musical problems (such as [dj-buddy](https://dfilaretti.github.io/dj-buddy/), a DJ playlist builder written in Haskell). Other times, I just keep them separate.

This website is where I write about things I find interesting, mostly programming, computer science, and occasionally music. It's also a place to share projects I've been working on.

You can also check out my [LinkedIn profile](https://www.linkedin.com/in/danfilaretti/) or download my [CV](../assets/downloads/Daniele%20Filaretti%20CV%202026.pdf).

Thanks for stopping by!
